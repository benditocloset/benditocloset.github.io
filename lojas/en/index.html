Frequently asked questions

How long does it take?

Up to 2 weeks from receiving all materials. If materials arrive late, the timeline adjusts.

How do I pay?

50% at the start and 50% at handover. Pay online securely with the online payment buttons (card, MB WAY, Multibanco, Apple Pay, Google Pay or Klarna), or by bank transfer.

Are there monthly costs?

There’s no platform fee. You only pay for the domain each year and, if you want them, professional email and maintenance.

What if I want changes later?

2 rounds of changes and 30 days of support are included. After that, you can choose monthly maintenance or one-off requests.

Do you work with clients outside Portugal?

Yes, remotely, in Portuguese, English or Spanish. For press outside Portugal, any mailing-service costs are extra. Ask for a quote.
